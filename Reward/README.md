## Challenge Description

> I receive a reward or mentioned on a wall of fame when i found vulnerability . I am participating in ..... program? Format of Flag ( Do not use spaces)

**Submission requirement:** No spaces in the flag.

## Solution

The description refers to a **bug bounty** program. In such programs, organizations invite researchers to report security vulnerabilities, sometimes offering monetary rewards or public recognition on a hall or wall of fame.

Because the challenge requires an answer without spaces, the phrase `bug bounty` becomes `bugbounty`.

## Key Takeaways

- Bug bounty programs encourage vulnerability reporting within a defined scope and rules.
- Recognition or rewards may be provided for eligible reports.
- Respect the challenge's exact answer format.
